💧 Fountain Codes Explained

Loss-tolerant encoding for frame drops

Frame Loss Tolerance: 33% (1.5× redundancy)
Encoding Type: Luby Transform (LT)
Distribution: Robust Soliton

Fountain codes allow the decoder to reconstruct the original data from any subset of encoded frames. You don't need to capture every single QR code - just enough of them!

Example:

With 10 source blocks and 1.5× redundancy, the encoder generates 15 frames. You only need to successfully scan ~10-12 frames (any of them) to reconstruct the original file.
